01 — Project Overview

Why Was It Built?

Don Mac Cafe faced real operational challenges before this system existed.

⏳
Long Queues
Peak-hour walk-in crowds caused customers to leave without ordering.
✏️
Manual Errors
Hand-written orders led to mistakes in quantities, items, and pricing.
📵
No Oversight
No digital way to monitor orders or revenue without physical presence.
💵
Cash Only
Zero support for GCash or Maya — only physical cash was accepted.
📉
No Records
No order history, making sales analysis and top-item tracking impossible.
02 / 11
02 — Beneficiaries

Who Benefits?

Every stakeholder gains something specific from this system.

👤 Customer / Guest
Convenience
Order from any device — no waiting in line, live status tracking, printable receipt.
🔐 Registered User
Personalization
Order history, reorder past meals instantly, manage profile and saved address.
🛠️ Cafe Staff / Admin
Control
Manage all orders in real time, update statuses, receive audio + notification alerts.
📊 Cafe Owner
Visibility
Revenue stats, top-selling items chart, payment method breakdown — all in the dashboard.
🚪 Guest (No Account)
Zero Barrier
Browse menu and place orders without registering — cart restores if they sign up later.
